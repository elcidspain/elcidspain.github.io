PHASE 2 — BUILD AN IMAGINE.2 PACK PER READY SOURCE

- exact job record;

PHASE 3 — IMAGINE.2 MOTION
Default render:
- 6 seconds, 24 fps;
- preserve original aspect ratio;
- optical push 1–3%, OR <=0.25 m virtual translation only when localization supports it;
- yaw <=3 degrees;
- no orbit, backside reveal, threshold crossing or door opening;
- no added people, furniture, doors, paths, windows, structures, trees, lights, landscaping;
